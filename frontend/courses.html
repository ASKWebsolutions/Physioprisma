Project of Physioprisma

Physioprisma Academy

Professional training & clinical education

Physioprisma Academy is the educational division of Physioprisma, dedicated to advancing clinical knowledge and professional development in healthcare. We offer nationally and internationally recognized training programs, certification courses, workshops, and seminars.

Certification Programs

Accredited diplomas and certificates in physiotherapy, aesthetics, healthcare management, clinical procedures and treatments and much more.

Workshops & Seminars

Hands-on clinical workshops led by UK-certified and international faculty, focused on practical skill-building.

Online Learning

Flexible webinars and self-paced digital courses accessible to healthcare professionals worldwide.

Healthcare Training Courses

Structured training in patient management, clinical reasoning, clinical procedures & treatments and healthcare administration.

Certification

Dry Cupping Therapy - Level 1 & 2

A two-day, hands-on certification that takes you from the fundamentals of cupping through to advanced combination protocols, with supervised practice on real cases and clear guidance on contraindications, skin response and aftercare.

  • Duration: 2 days (16 hours) of supervised practical training
  • Format: Live demonstrations, paired practice and case-based scenarios
  • Core techniques: Static, sliding, flash and dynamic cupping
  • Safety: Contraindications, skin assessment, hygiene and aftercare
  • Who it's for: Physiotherapists, massage therapists and sports coaches
  • Certification: Physioprisma Academy certificate awarded on completion

Advanced

Dry Needling - Basic to Advanced

A structured, safety-first pathway from dry needling fundamentals to advanced trigger point work. Across three days you build accurate palpation, muscle selection and needle handling skills, then apply them under direct supervision.

  • Duration: 3 days (24 hours) of theory and supervised needling practice
  • Format: Classroom teaching with live practice on models and volunteers
  • Core skills: Trigger point identification, depth, angle and needle handling
  • Safety: Sterile technique, contraindications and adverse-event management
  • Who it's for: Licensed physiotherapists and final-year DPT students
  • Certification: Internationally recognized certificate on completion

Includes Kit

K-Taping / Kinesiology Taping Certification

A two-day, application-focused workshop that teaches you to tape with purpose rather than by pattern - correction, functional and lymphatic techniques for pain relief, oedema control, posture and sports performance.

  • Duration: 2 days (16 hours) of application-focused workshop training
  • Format: Demonstration, guided practice and correction on real volunteers
  • Core techniques: Muscle, joint, ligament and lymphatic taping
  • Applications: Pain relief, oedema control, posture and sports performance
  • Who it's for: Physiotherapists, chiropractors and athletic trainers
  • Includes: Taping kit and Physioprisma Academy certification

CPD Points

Manual Therapy Techniques

Three days of advanced hands-on training in joint mobilization, manipulation, soft tissue work and myofascial release, applied to real musculoskeletal cases across the cervical, thoracic, lumbar and extremity joints.

  • Duration: 3 days (24 hours) of practical and theory sessions
  • Format: Lecture, demonstration and one-to-one skill assessment
  • Core techniques: Mobilization, manipulation, soft tissue and myofascial release
  • Focus: Cervical, thoracic, lumbar and extremity joints
  • Who it's for: Practicing physiotherapists seeking advanced clinical skills
  • Certification: CPD points awarded with an Academy certificate

Field Training

Sports Injury Management & Taping

Two days of field-based training covering the full return-to-sport pathway: assessment, acute management, taping, phased rehabilitation and testing - built for clinicians and trainers who make decisions on the sideline.

  • Duration: 2 days (16 hours) of field-based practical training
  • Format: On-field assessment drills, taping labs and rehab planning
  • Core skills: Assessment, acute management, taping and rehab progression
  • Focus: Return-to-sport testing and load management strategies
  • Who it's for: Sports physiotherapists, trainers and team support staff
  • Certification: Physioprisma Academy certificate on completion
View Course
Case Based

Pediatric Physiotherapy Essentials

Two days of interactive, case-based training in developmental assessment, neurodevelopmental treatment and play-based therapy, designed for pediatric physiotherapists and DPT students.

  • Duration: 2 days (16 hours) of interactive, case-based training
  • Format: Small-group workshops with live demonstration and role play
  • Core skills: Developmental assessment and standardized observation
  • Techniques: Neurodevelopmental treatment and play-based therapy
  • Who it's for: Pediatric physiotherapists and DPT students
  • Certification: Physioprisma Academy certificate on completion
