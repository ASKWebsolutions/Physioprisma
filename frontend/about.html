About Us

Physioprisma is Pakistan's integrated healthcare organization - uniting clinical care, professional healthcare education, and premium treatment equipments under one trusted name. Everything we offer is built by practicing clinicians, for patients and professionals alike.

Founder & CEO

Dr. Tuba Sultan

Consultant Physiotherapist | Clinical Researcher
2+ Years of Clinical & Leadership Experience

Dr. Tuba Sultan is a Consultant Physiotherapist graduated from the United College of Physical Therapy, a Certified Aesthetic Practitioner, Clinical Researcher, and licensed ACLS & BLS provider. As the Founder & CEO of Physioprisma, she is dedicated to delivering ethical, evidence-based, and patient-centered rehabilitation care, with a strong focus on professionalism, compassion, and clinical excellence.

Her clinical expertise spans pain management and rehabilitation across orthopedic, musculoskeletal, neurological, gynaecological, pediatric, and geriatric conditions. She brings hands-on experience in patient care, and healthcare management, always prioritizing recovery, and long-term wellness.

Beyond direct patient care, Dr. Tuba actively contributes to improving healthcare accessibility by ensuring that both professionals and patients have access to clinically reliable treatment solutions and high-quality physiotherapy equipment.

She is equally passionate about clinical education and professional development. Dr. Tuba is developed a learning platform for healthcare professionals that promotes practical training, clinical skill-building, and continuous professional education through hands-on learning initiatives including workshops and courses.

Committed to innovation in physiotherapy practice, she integrates modern rehabilitation approaches and evidence-based techniques to advance standards of care and improve patient outcomes across the field.

Co-Founder & Director

Dr. Syed Sultan Ahmed Zaidi

Consultant Physiotherapist in Medicare cardiac & general hospital | Rehabilitation Specialist 4+ Years of Experience

Dr. Syed Sultan Ahmed Zaidi is a dedicated Consultant Physiotherapist from Ziauddin University. He is a UK-certified practitioner of Dry Needling and Dynamic Cupping. With over four years of hands-on clinical experience in musculoskeletal, neurological, cardiac, orthopaedic, and sports rehabilitation, he is committed to helping patients regain mobility, reduce pain, and improve quality of life through evidence-based, patient-centered care.

As Co-Founder & Director of Physioprisma, Dr. Zaidi is driven by a belief in clinical excellence, continuous learning, and accessible rehabilitation for all. His approach combines modern physiotherapy techniques with compassionate patient management, ensuring every treatment plan is tailored to the individual needs of each patient.

In addition to his clinical role, Dr. Zaidi is actively involved in developing a learning platform for healthcare professionals under the umbrella of Physioprisma, focused on clinical skill development, hands-on training, and continuous medical education to advance physiotherapy practice.
