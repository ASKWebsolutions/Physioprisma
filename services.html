Our Physiotherapy Services

Personalized, evidence-based treatments designed to relieve pain, restore function, and improve quality of life - without surgery or long-term medication.

Manual Therapy

Hands-on techniques to reduce pain, improve joint mobility, and restore normal movement patterns.

  • Joint mobilization & manipulation
  • Soft tissue release & myofascial work
  • Effective for back/neck pain, stiffness & posture issues
Book Manual Therapy Session

Dry Needling

Targeted trigger point release using fine needles to relieve muscle tightness, reduce pain fast, and improve function.

  • Ideal for chronic muscle pain & knots
  • Complements manual therapy
  • Quick relief with minimal downtime
Book Dry Needling

Dry Cupping (Static & Dynamic)

Suction-based therapy to improve blood flow, release fascial restrictions, reduce inflammation, and relieve deep tissue tension.

  • Static cups for targeted areas
  • Dynamic (sliding) for larger muscle groups
  • Excellent for back, shoulder & leg pain
Book Cupping Session

K-Taping / Kinesiology Taping

Elastic therapeutic taping to support muscles/joints, reduce pain, improve circulation, and enhance performance without restricting movement.

  • Used for injury prevention & recovery
  • Supports posture & joint stability
  • Water-resistant & comfortable for daily use
Book K-Taping

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Comprehensive programs to treat and prevent sports-related injuries, restore strength, flexibility, and return to sport safely.

  • ACL, ankle sprains, shoulder issues & more
  • Functional training & performance enhancement
  • Collaboration with fitness centers
Book Sports Rehab

Pediatric Physiotherapy

Gentle, play-based therapy for children with developmental delays, cerebral palsy, torticollis, or post-injury needs.

  • Improves motor skills & coordination
  • Child-friendly environment & approach
  • Family education & home programs
Book Pediatric Session
